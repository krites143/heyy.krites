2. What Information We Collect

We collect information only when you actively choose to submit an enquiry through the contact form on our website. The fields in that form include:

  • Full name
  • Mobile number
  • State and district
  • Your role or interest (e.g. distributor, farmer, investor)
  • Your message

We do not store this information in a database on this website. When you submit the form, the data is pre-populated into a WhatsApp message or email draft in your own device’s app — you must then press send yourself. We only receive what you choose to send.

We also collect standard server log data (IP address, browser type, referring URL) automatically when you visit our site. This is standard web server behaviour and is not linked to any personal profile.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information you send to us via the contact form is used solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry (partnership, distribution, farming, investment, media, or general).
  • Connect you with the appropriate Kotwari representative.
  • Follow up on your specific request or area of interest.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Messages are passed directly to our team via WhatsApp or email — we do not intercept or re-process them through any third-party CRM unless we notify you otherwise.

4. Cookies and Analytics

This website currently does not set any first-party cookies for tracking, advertising, or analytics purposes.

A single browser localStorage key (kotwari_cookie_consent) is stored on your device to remember your cookie consent preference. This value never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

The hero image on our homepage is loaded from Unsplash (unsplash.com). Visiting Unsplash-hosted images may result in a request to their CDN, which has its own privacy policy at unsplash.com/privacy.

Google Maps links on this site open in Google’s own service. If you click those links, Google’s privacy policy applies.

If we add analytics (such as Google Analytics or Plausible) in the future, this policy will be updated before those services are activated, and appropriate consent mechanisms will be provided.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website integrates with or links to the following external services, each governed by its own data policies:

  • Unsplash — image content delivery network used for certain website images.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — used as an optional enquiry delivery channel. Messages are handled by WhatsApp’s platform once you press send.
  • Google Maps — linked for address and location reference. Opening Google Maps is subject to Google’s privacy policy.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these services if you have concerns about their data practices.
