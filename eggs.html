QUALITY VERIFICATION

From Village Nest to Urban Table

A four-stage monitored cold chain system delivering superior yolk firmness, zero cracked shells, and verified freshness.

Stage 01

Bio-Secure Laying

Laid in automated climate-controlled coops by hens nourished with veterinarian-balanced maize and natural calcium.

Stage 02

Electronic Candling

High-speed optical candling screens out hairline cracks and validates dense, high-standing yolk centers.

Stage 03

UV Sanitization & Pack

Microbial surface sanitization without chemical washes, immediately sealed into shock-absorbing molded pulp trays.

Stage 04

24-Hour Cold Fleet

Temperature-logged dispatch fleet distributes directly to partner retail points, eliminating urban middleman storage.
