Technology in Rural Hands

Unique Kotwari Farmer ID System

Every registered farmer is assigned a unique alphanumeric digital identity (e.g. KOT-UP-LKO-000142) linked to a QR-coded digital passbook.

  • Farmer Profile: Landholding, bird capacity, cattle count, and active agreement status.
  • Daily Collection Logs: Real-time records of eggs supplied, rejected counts, or milk litres with fat percentages.
  • Financial Transparency: Clear view of weekly payouts, pending balances, input debits, and credit ledger.
  • Data Privacy: Bank account and sensitive personal records secured strictly behind authorized roles.
Digital ID Format: KOT (Kotwari) — UP (State) — LKO (District) — 000001 (Farmer Sequence)
