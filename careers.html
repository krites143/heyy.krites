Dairy & Processing Full-time

Quality Assurance & Cold-Chain Technologist

📍 Kursi Road Processing Facility, Lucknow

Lead microbiological analysis, automated rapid chilling verification, Clean-In-Place (CIP) sanitation protocols, and statutory FSSAI compliance audits.

  • Degree in Dairy Technology / Food Science / Microbiology
  • Proficiency in SNF/Fat testing and adulteration diagnostics
  • Familiarity with NABL & HACCP documentation standards
Global Trade & Exports Full-time / Hybrid

Export Logistics & Documentation Executive

📍 Corporate Office, Lucknow / Hybrid

Coordinate APEDA inspection dossiers, phytosanitary certifications, container ocean freight allocations (JNPT/Mundra), and GCC customs compliance.

  • Bachelor's in Foreign Trade, Logistics, or Commerce
  • Knowledge of Incoterms 2020 (CIF, FOB, CFR) and Letters of Credit
  • 3+ years with agro-commodity shipping lines or clearing agents

Enterprise Overview

Functional Divisions at Kotwari

We operate 12 core functional domains supporting our rural-to-global mandate.

Village Procurement

Electronic weigh station management, farmer aggregation, and fair pricing enforcement.

Dairy Operations

Rapid chilling hubs, bulk milk transport, quality grading, and pasteurized packaging.

Poultry & Eggs

Sahebganj layer management, hygienic candling, grading, and protective egg carton packaging.

FMCG & Milling

Cold-pressed oil ghani operations, spice stone-grinding, and pure chana sattu roasting.

Cold Chain Logistics

Refrigerated fleet dispatch, reefer maintenance, and route temperature data-logging.

Global Trade Desk

International container fulfillment, APEDA & Halal compliance, and buyer negotiations.

Kotwari One Retail

Convenience franchise management and high-protein breakfast point operations.

Quality & Lab Safety

NABL-traceable microbiological tests, pesticide residue analysis, and FSSAI standards.

Finance & Audit

Statutory compliance, direct farmer payments, and transparent ledger operations.

Digital Infrastructure

Farmer mobile records, weighbridge electronic integration, and inventory software.

Brand & Marketing

Packaging design, rural-urban brand storytelling, and trade exhibition presentations.

Human Resources

Talent recruitment, field training programs, workplace ethics, and employee welfare.
