Anchor Case Study

Sahebganj Eggs Producer Organisation Ltd.

Sahebganj Eggs Producer Organisation Ltd. represents Kotwari's flagship model of transforming fragmented backyard and small shed poultry farmers into a disciplined commercial enterprise.

  • Bulk Feed & Medication Sourcing: Reduced flock mortality and lowered feed costs through bulk procurement agreements.
  • Centralized Collection Centre: Village-level temperature-managed facility for automated grading and candling.
  • Direct Value Realization: Farmer members receive guaranteed base pricing with performance bonuses for egg weight and shell strength.
  • Brand Premium: Sourced eggs are packaged as Kotwari Premium Eggs & Arabian Collection for tier-1 supermarket and export sale.
