The Kotwari Dairy Model

Farmer to Chilling to Brand: An Integrated Value Chain

India's small dairy farmers have traditionally suffered from fragmented middlemen and opaque fat testing. Kotwari's digital collection framework guarantees transparent digital lactometer readings, fair MSP pricing, and prompt bank transfers.

01. Dairy Farmer → 02. Village Collection Centre → 03. Automated Fat/SNF Testing → 04. 4°C Chilling Tank → 05. Processing & Packaging → 06. Cold-Chain Retail
  • Digital Milk Collection Ledger: Immediate printout of litres, Fat %, SNF %, and total payout value.
  • Cattle Healthcare Coordination: Veterinary vaccination drives and nutritious balanced cattle feed support.
  • FPO Integration: Enabling Women Dairy Self-Help Groups and Producer Companies to aggregate at scale.

Quality From Cow to Cup

Zero Compromise on Purity and Hygiene

Every litre of milk received at Kotwari collection facilities undergoes strict quality protocols before pasteurisation and packing:

  • Automated Adulteration Screening: Checking for urea, detergent, starch, added water, and neutralizers.
  • Microbiological Safety: Rapid MBRT and somatic cell count testing to ensure clean udder hygiene.
  • Multi-stage Pasteurisation: High-Temperature Short-Time (HTST) pasteurisation destroying harmful pathogens while protecting heat-sensitive vitamins and fresh taste.
  • Tamper-Evident Packaging: Food-grade oxygen-barrier pouches and hermetically induction-sealed tubs for Deshi Ghee.
Food Safety Note: Kotwari Dairy processes milk adhering to statutory FSSAI standards. Detailed laboratory test certificates and nutritional panels are available upon request for institutional and export buyers.
