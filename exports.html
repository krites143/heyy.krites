Where we aim to grow.

🇦🇪 UAE & GCC

Middle East and Gulf region present strong hospitality and retail demand for quality Indian food products. Indian expat communities and premium hospitality channels are key target segments.

🌏 South & Southeast Asia

Regional food trade corridors and large Indian diaspora markets in countries like Singapore, Malaysia and Sri Lanka offer natural distribution pathways for Indian agricultural products.

🌍 Africa & Other Markets

Emerging markets and Indian food export corridors in East and West Africa represent medium-term expansion opportunities, particularly for staples and packaged goods.

These are target markets. Confirmed export operations and market-specific product eligibility will be communicated directly by our team.

Products

Products with export potential

🥚 Eggs

Quality-graded, packaged eggs for international markets, subject to destination country import regulations, phytosanitary requirements and FSSAI standards.

🌾 Rice & Staples

Basmati rice, atta (wheat flour), poha and other Indian staple food products for export to diaspora and general retail markets internationally.

🌶️ Spices

Haldi (turmeric), red chilli, garam masala and spice blends sourced from Indian growing regions for export to hospitality, retail and food service buyers.

🥜 Snacks & Namkeen

Regional speciality snacks and namkeen products for Indian diaspora communities and Indian food retail internationally.

💧 KooA Mineral Water

Premium KooA water for international hospitality, travel and retail — with Arabic-inspired design positioning for Gulf and Middle East markets.

🌱 Other Agricultural Products

Other Indian agricultural products subject to export licence, phytosanitary certificates and destination market approvals. Contact us for specific enquiries.

Export eligibility, certifications and packaging requirements vary by destination. Contact our team for product-specific enquiries.
