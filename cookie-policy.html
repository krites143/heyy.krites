Cookie Policy

How Kotwari International uses cookies and browser storage.

Last updated: 14 September 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you browse websites. They help websites recognize your preferences and provide seamless browsing experiences.

2. What We Store

Currently, Kotwari International does not store tracking cookies or intrusive identifiers on your machine. We use lightweight browser storage (localStorage) strictly for storing your cookie consent preference (kotwari_cookie_consent), ensuring we do not prompt you repeatedly after you make a choice.

3. Analytics & Third-Party Services

When Google Analytics or privacy-respecting analytics tools are active, optional statistical cookies help us understand aggregate traffic metrics (e.g. pages visited, referral sources) without identifying individual personal identities. We enable IP anonymization on all telemetry tools.

External links to services such as WhatsApp or Google Maps operate under their respective privacy and cookie policies when visited.

4. Managing Your Preferences

You can accept or decline cookie preferences using the cookie banner displayed on the homepage. You can also clear cookies or stored site data at any time through your browser's privacy settings.
