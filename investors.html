About

About Kotwari International

Kotwari International Ltd. is building an integrated agriculture, food, FMCG, distribution and retail ecosystem that connects village production to organised markets across India and internationally.

Operating through multiple business verticals — from farmer-integrated egg supply to premium mineral water, dairy, FMCG, organised retail and export — Kotwari is designed to create value at every point in the agricultural supply chain.

Our model is rooted in rural India: sourcing from farmers and farmer producer organisations (FPOs), adding value through processing, grading, branding and packaging, and connecting this production to wider distribution, retail and export markets.

  • Farmer-integrated supply chains
  • Multiple business verticals
  • Kotwari Eggs, KooA Water, Dairy, FMCG, Retail & Exports
  • Brand-to-market integration
  • Rural entrepreneurship focus
  • FPO and collection centre network
  • Quality grading and branded packaging
  • Institutional and export channel development
Model

How Kotwari creates value

Production → Aggregation → Collection → Quality Addition → Brand → Distribution → Retail → Consumer → Export
Layer 1

Production

Farmer and FPO network supplying eggs, dairy, grains, spices and other agricultural products.

Layer 2

Value Addition

Processing, grading, packaging and branding that transform raw production into market-ready products.

Layer 3

Market Access

Retail, distribution, institutional and export channels that deliver Kotwari products to end consumers.
