Chairman's Message

“India's Villages Must Participate in the Value They Create.”

For generations, the Indian farmer has been relegated to the weakest position in the commercial chain — selling raw harvest under distress and buying back packaged products at multiples of the cost.

At Kotwari, we are building an integrated model where our rural producers are not passive suppliers, but co-participants in organized production, quality grading, modern branding, cold-chain distribution, and market growth.

Whether it is a layer poultry farmer in Sahebganj, a dairy keeper on Kursi Road, or a mustard cultivator in rural Awadh, our goal is clear: to ensure their produce is respected, graded fairly, and carried with pride across India and outward to the world.
